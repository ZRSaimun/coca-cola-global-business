# Coca-Cola: Global Business Strategy

A phone-friendly companion to our BUSI-1763 group mind map. Explore the A1 poster, four module lenses, the group conclusion and the sources behind the analysis.

**Live presentation:** https://zrsaimun.github.io/coca-cola-global-business/

This is a student group project, not an official Coca-Cola publication.

The presentation includes a six-chapter, scroll-driven value-chain film (ingredients → bottle production → filling → capping and labelling → local distribution → customer), 48 illustrative frames in `assets/bottle-journey/film/`. Scrolling acts as a playhead: frames dissolve into each other and the camera pans across the wide shots. Without JavaScript, or with reduced motion turned on, the same chapters appear as an illustrated list. Journey images are illustrative, not photographs of Coca-Cola facilities.
